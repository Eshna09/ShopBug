## Issue Report

> The subtotal in my cart is way too low. I have two items at $49.99 each but it's
> showing $51.99 instead of $99.98.

## Expected Behavior

Cart subtotal = sum of `(product.price × quantity)` for every item.
Two items at $49.99 each → subtotal = $99.98.

## Actual Behavior

Cart subtotal = sum of `(product.price + quantity)` for every item.
Two items at $49.99 each → subtotal = $51.99 (price + 2 instead of price × 2).

## Root Cause Description

`Cart.jsx` line 63, the `reduce` callback:
```js
// BUG
sum + item.product.price + item.quantity
// CORRECT
sum + item.product.price * item.quantity
```
The `+` operator is used instead of `*`, treating quantity as an addend rather than a
multiplier. The error scales: a single item at $100 with quantity=3 gives $103 instead of $300.

## Reproduction Steps

1. Log in and navigate to the Products page.
2. Add 2 units of any product to the cart.
3. Open the Cart page.
4. Observe the Subtotal — it shows `price + 2` rather than `price × 2`.
