## Issue Report

I was able to add more units of a product to my cart than were available in stock.

## Expected Behavior

Adding to cart with quantity exceeding product stock should be rejected with HTTP 400.

## Actual Behavior

The stock check was removed from add_to_cart, allowing quantities exceeding stock.

## Root Cause

- **File**: app/services/cart_service.py
- **Function**: add_to_cart
- **Lines**: 12, 13
- **Affected Files**: app/services/cart_service.py
