# Bug Report: B014

- **Category**: logic
- **Difficulty**: hard
- **Issue Report Quality**: vague
- **Scope**: multi-file

## Issue Report

Even with a discount cap set, the discount sometimes exceeds the cap amount.

## Expected Behavior

The discount cap should limit the computed percentage discount, not be compared against the subtotal.

## Actual Behavior

The cap is compared against the subtotal before computing the percentage, so the cap either always applies (when subtotal > cap) or never applies (when subtotal <= cap), regardless of the actual discount amount.

## Root Cause

- **File**: app/services/discount_service.py
- **Function**: calculate_discount_amount
- **Lines**: 20, 21
- **Affected Files**: app/services/discount_service.py, app/services/checkout_service.py

### Description

The max-cap check compares subtotal against max_discount_amount instead of comparing the computed discount (raw) against the cap, neutering the cap logic.

## Reproduction Steps

  1. Create a discount with 50% percent and max_discount_amount=20.
  2. Call calculate_discount_amount with subtotal=30.
  3. Observe that the discount is 20 (cap) instead of 15 (correct computed amount).
