## Issue Report

> When I click on Cart in the navigation I see my order history instead of my cart.
> And when I click Orders I get the cart page. The two pages are completely swapped.

## Expected Behavior

- Navigating to `/cart` renders the Cart page.
- Navigating to `/orders` renders the Orders page.

## Actual Behavior

- Navigating to `/cart` renders the Orders page.
- Navigating to `/orders` renders the Cart page.

## Root Cause Description

`App.jsx` lines 19–20 have the route `element` props swapped:
```jsx
// BUG
<Route path="/cart"   element={<Orders />} />
<Route path="/orders" element={<Cart />}   />
// CORRECT
<Route path="/cart"   element={<Cart />}   />
<Route path="/orders" element={<Orders />} />
```
The components are imported correctly but assigned to the wrong paths.

## Reproduction Steps

1. Open the ShopBug frontend.
2. Click **Cart** in the navigation bar.
3. Observe the Orders page is displayed.
4. Click **Orders** in the navigation bar.
5. Observe the Cart page is displayed.
